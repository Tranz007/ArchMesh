# Code of Conduct

ArchMesh is intended to be a constructive, practical open-source project. Contributors should be able to discuss product, UX, architecture, code, and tradeoffs candidly without personal hostility.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas, implementations, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good intent, but do not use that assumption to dismiss real impact.
- Keep reviews focused on improving the project.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of experience and different technical/design backgrounds.
- Do not harass, threaten, demean, discriminate against, or deliberately intimidate other participants.

## Project discussions

Strong technical and design disagreement is allowed. ArchMesh explicitly values challenge, blindspot detection, and evidence over easy consensus. Keep that disagreement grounded in the work.

## Enforcement

The repository owner may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately rather than posting personal or identifying details in a public issue.
